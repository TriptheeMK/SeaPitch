Designed by Arquitectonica as SM Prime Holdings’ 56th mall, SM Seaside City sits at the heart of Cebu’s South Road Properties — a 30-hectare mixed-use masterplan that will ultimately include residences, offices, a five-star hotel, the 16,000-seat SM Arena Seaside (opening June 2026), and the SMX Convention Center. Metro Cebu’s fastest-growing commercial corridor, drawing shoppers from across the Visayas and Mindanao North.
