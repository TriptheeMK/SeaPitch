Luxury & Premium

Cebu’s Rising Premium Address.

SM Seaside City is the anchor of Cebu’s South Road Properties masterplan — a corridor where premium residential, office, and hospitality assets are rapidly co-locating. As SRP matures, so does the calibre of its retail adjacency.

An active Audi dealership is already in-place. Charles & Keith, The North Face, Timberland, and a growing premium lifestyle roster set the tone. Ground-floor flagships with maximum visibility and foot-traffic are available now.

Audi Cebu Charles & Keith The North Face Timberland American Eagle SM Director’s Club Tim Ho Wan Starbucks Reserve

As SM Seaside Cebu Arena opens (June 2026, 16,000 fixed seats / 25,000 full-house), post-concert F&B and luxury impulse traffic will surge. Premium brands entering now establish category first-mover advantage in the Visayas’ fastest-growing SRP corridor.
