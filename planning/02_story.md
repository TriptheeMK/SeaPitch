# Step 2 — The Sales Story (5 points)

This is a sales deck, not a tourism site. Every line below points at leasing, sponsorship, or event-booking revenue.

## 1. Why this property is special

SM Seaside City is not a mall — it's the Visayas' single largest commercial gravity well. Circular architecture, a rooftop Sky Park, IMAX, an Olympic ice rink, and a 1,857 m² convention hall, all under one 470,000 m² roof on Cebu's South Road Properties.

## 2. Why brands should lease here

700+ tenants already trade here because the catchment is regional, not local — Visayas, Mindanao North, and a steady international tourist flow through Mactan. A flagship at Seaside reaches a market no standalone Cebu storefront can match.

## 3. Why sponsors should partner here

Sky Park, the main atrium, and the circular concourse are purpose-built activation canvases. Sponsors get cinema lobbies, an ice rink with year-round programming, and an audience that spans families, students, luxury shoppers, and tourists — segmented and addressable.

## 4. Why event organizers should book here

Sky Hall Seaside Cebu offers 1,857 m² of flexible space across two halls — concerts, expos, product launches, corporate galas. Backed by SMX Convention Center operations, with built-in foot traffic from the mall below. No separate venue logistics needed.

## 5. Why this has more scale than a normal mall

470,486 m² of GFA. The largest mall in the Visayas, top-4 in the Philippines. A rooftop park with sea and mountain views, a 500-seat IMAX, an 18-lane bowling alley, an Olympic ice rink, a convention hall, and seven cinemas — this operates like a small city, not a shopping center.

## Rules being followed

- Each point is 1–3 sentences, not paragraphs.
- Story is anchored to business action (lease / sponsor / book), not tourism.
- Tourist features (Sky Park, IMAX) are framed as commercial assets, not attractions to "visit."
