# Step 3 — Section Plan (locked before design/code)

7 sections. Non-linear nav — viewer jumps to any module from the persistent menu. Each section has a distinct visual treatment so the deck doesn't feel uniform.

## 1. Hero — Cinematic open

- Background video: aerial / circular-architecture reveal, Sky Park sunset
- One headline, one sub-line, single CTA pointing at Events or Leasing
- First 10 seconds must communicate: scale, energy, the Visayas
- Treatment: full-bleed video, minimal chrome, dark UI

## 2. Why This Property

- Location map (SRP / Cebu / Visayas)
- Animated counters: 470,486 m² · 700+ tenants · regional reach
- Demographics + visitor profile
- Treatment: data-driven, light UI, motion-on-scroll

## 3. Retail + Luxury

- Tenant logo wall (anchors first, then notable retail)
- Luxury wing positioning — separate visual tier with darker palette
- Category breakdown: department, supermarket, fashion, luxury, F&B-adjacent
- Treatment: editorial grid, large product/lifestyle imagery

## 4. Dining & Lifestyle

- Food as destination, not afterthought
- Restaurant rows / hero plate imagery / lifestyle moments
- Treatment: warm palette, slower pacing, video b-roll loops

## 5. Attractions & Entertainment

- IMAX, Super Screen, Sky Park, ice rink, bowling — the differentiators
- Each as a tile that expands into video + short story
- Treatment: high-energy, fast transitions, big video

## 6. Events & Platform

- Sky Hall (1,857 m²) hero spec sheet
- Past activations / concerts / Sinulog imagery
- Booking CTA inline
- Treatment: confident, dark, capacity numbers front and center

## 7. Contact / Inquiry CTA

- Three paths: Lease a space · Sponsor an activation · Book the venue
- Each path opens its own form / mailto with pre-filled subject
- Treatment: clean, focused, no other navigation noise

## Phase-2 sub-modules (architected for, built if time allows)

- `/events` — Sky Hall deep-dive, floor plans from capacity sheet
- `/sponsorship` — tiers, audience data, activation case studies
- `/leasing` — split by luxury / retail / F&B / pop-up

## Rules being followed

- 7 sections — not over-stuffed.
- Each section gets a distinct treatment (palette, pacing, density).
- Reads as a deck, not a blog: every section ends in or implies a CTA.
