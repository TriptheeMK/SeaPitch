# Step 1 — Subject & Assets

**Subject (locked):** SM Seaside City Cebu

## Basic facts

- **Location:** South Road Properties (SRP), Cebu City, Philippines
- **Scale:** Gross floor area ~470,486 m²; gross leasable area 200,000+ m²
- **Ranking:** 3rd–4th largest mall in the Philippines; largest in the Visayas
- **Tenants:** 700+ stores and services
- **Anchors:** Two-level SM Store (~20,389 m²), SM Supermarket, Hypermarket (~12,410 m²)
- **Hours:** 11:00–21:00 weekdays, 10:00–22:00 weekends

## Major attractions

- **Sky Park** — elevated rooftop garden with sea and mountain panoramas; observation tower
- **IMAX Theater** — 500+ seats, top-tier digital projection
- **Centerstage / Super Screen Cinema** — premium screen format
- **2 Director's Club + 4 regular cinemas**
- **Olympic-sized ice skating rink**
- **18-lane bowling center**
- **Circular architecture** — signature radial floor plan, instantly recognizable

## Event & venue capacity

- **Sky Hall Seaside Cebu** — 3rd-level venue, two halls, 1,857 m² flexible space for exhibits, corporate functions, social events (operated by SMX Convention Center)
